# P1: synthetic demo documents (freeze item 12)

FR-03, FR-04, NFR-15, NFR-17, NFR-18. Replaces the skip recorded in D-007.

## Built
- `tools/fixtures/specs.ts`: ten fixtures defined once, each producing its HTML and its answer key.
- `tools/make-fixtures.ts` (`npm run fixtures`): Playwright with an installed Chrome renders PDFs and screenshots. sharp degrades the photos (tilt, shear, uneven lighting, seeded noise, camera softness, JPEG compression).
- `tools/fixtures/keys/*.json` (ten keys), validated by the existing `key-schema.ts`.
- `tools/fixtures/README.md`: the fixture table, what each tests, what they do not prove, and the line asking for two real phone photos.
- `tests/fixtures.test.ts` (32): keys validate; every key string is on its page verbatim; footer on every page; coverage of freeze item 12; dd/mm/yyyy in eight documents and ISO in one; raster images 500 KB or less and 1600 px or less; PDF page counts; names marked SYNTHETIC.
